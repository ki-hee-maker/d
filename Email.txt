1. Open Gmail.
2. Open the sample email.
3. Click 3 dots (beside Reply) > Show original.
4. Click Copy to clipboard.
5. Open:
   https://toolbox.googleapps.com/apps/messageheader/
6. Paste the complete header > Analyse.
7. Note: sender, receiver, date, mail server, SPF, DKIM, DMARC and IP.

IP DETAILS:
8. Copy the IP from the header.
9. Open https://ipinfo.io/
10. Paste IP in search.
11. Note: country, city, ISP and organisation.

KALI:
whois IP_ADDRESS

WINDOWS:
nslookup DOMAIN_NAME
tracert DOMAIN_NAME
